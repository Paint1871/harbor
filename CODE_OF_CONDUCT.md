# Harbor community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Help people participate regardless
of experience, disability, age, identity, background, or beliefs. Critique work
with specific evidence, accept corrections, and respect boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, sharing
private information without consent, and repeated disruption are unacceptable.
These expectations apply to project discussions, reviews, issues, and spaces
where someone represents Harbor.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or exclude
participants when necessary. They should explain decisions, consider context,
and handle reports with discretion. A maintainer involved in a report should
not decide its outcome alone.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer using the contact method
on their public profile. Do not post private evidence in a public issue. If the
concern involves that maintainer, contact another maintainer where possible.
Reports should describe what happened and the response being requested.
